About Me

Educator at heart.
Engineer by craft.

I believe the best software is built by people who truly understand the humans who use it. My years teaching Computer Science, STEM, and Robotics in Myanmar gave me exactly that : a deep understanding of how people learn, struggle, and grow with technology.

In 2025, I moved to Singapore to pursue a Graduate Diploma in System Analysis at NUS-ISS, where I fell in love with full-stack engineering. I now build production-grade features at Magenta Consulting Services, working on an AI-powered SaaS platform used by real businesses.

My goal is to combine the empathy of an educator and the rigour of an engineer to build AI systems that make a genuine difference — especially in education across ASEAN.
